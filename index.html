About the Office

Building a Safer,
Better Community

The Municipal Engineering Office is the frontline government unit responsible for regulating construction activities, issuing building and related permits, and ensuring that all structures within the municipality comply with the National Building Code of the Philippines and local ordinances.

We are committed to delivering efficient, transparent, and citizen-centered services that protect public safety and support sustainable development in our community.

Building Permit Requirements

Applicable for new construction, renovation, addition, alteration, or repair of any structure.

A

Legal Documents

  • Updated Certified True Copy of Transfer Cert. of Title in LRA paper
  • Tax Clearance or RPT Receipt & Bill
  • Tax Declaration of the property
    1. a. Land
    2. b. Building (if renovation)
  • If lot is not in the name of the applicant:
    1. Deed of Absolute Sale
    2. Award Notice and/or Lease Contract
    3. Deed of Assignment or Donation
    4. Others
  • For Corporation
    1. Secretary's Certification Authorizing the Signatory
B

Clearances

  • Locational Clearance with Barangay Clearance
  • Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance (FSEC) & other Fire Safety Clearances
  • DOLE Construction Safety & Health Program
    1. Supplemental Documents (if applicable)
  • DPWH, DENR-ECC, CAAP (ATO), DOH-BHDT, HLURB, LGU, DOT, DOTC, DILG, PPA, DepED, PHIVOLCS, LLDA, MWSS, DAR, DA, DOLE, NHA, NCWDP and other agencies
  • Home Owner's Association Clearance (if subdivision)/if no HOA, Developer's Clearance
  • Recent Certificate of Occupancy of all existing buildings within the subject lot and one (1) complete set of approved building plans, if application is renovation / addition or extension
  • Power Lines Operator Certificate (PLOC)
  • Others
C

Technical Documents (application forms

  • Building Permit Form (NOTARIZED)
  • Sanitary/Plumbing Permit Form
  • Electrical PErmit Form
  • Mechanical Permit Form
  • Electronics PErmit Form
  • Photocopies of valid PRC IDs & current PTRs with dry-seal and 3 specimen signatures of the professionals
D

Supplemental Documents (if applicable)

  • Notarized Authorization Letter (for applicant's representative
  • Affidavit of Undertaking, Consent, etc.

Certificate of Occupancy / Use Requirements

Issued upon completion of a structure confirming it is safe and ready for use.

A

Clearances/Certificates/Legal Documents

  • Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC / original) / FSEC
  • Locational Clearance
  • Approved Building Permit (photocopy), present original copy if permit has no bar code
  • Green Building Certificate (if "GBO" covered)
  • Approved Sanitary (photocopy), present original copy if permit has no bar code
  • CEI (photocopy) or Approved Electrical Permit (photocopy) w/ CEI App Forms (1 copy)
  • Approved Mechanical Permit (photocopy, present original copy if permit has no bar code)
  • Corporate Secretary's Certification of a Board Resolution Authorizing the Signatory (if corporation / notarized) original copy)
    1. Others
B

Technical Documents (form must be duly accomplished, signed & sealed by professional / signed by the owner/s)

  • Certificate of Completion (for the structure / photocopy, wet signed / NOTARIZED)
  • As-built Structural Plans (signed and sealed)
  • Logbook of construction activities
  • Certificate of Final Inspection by Building Inspector
C

Clearances Required

  • Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (BFP)
  • Electrical Final Inspection Report (MERALCO/NAPOCOR)
  • Sanitary Clearance from Health Office
  • Barangay Clearance (no pending complaints)

Business Permit Requirements

Required for the operation of businesses and commercial establishments.

A

Basic Requirements

  • Duly accomplished Electrical Permit Application Form
  • Proof of property ownership or authorization from owner
  • Copy of approved Building Permit (if applicable)
  • Latest Real Property Tax Receipt
B

Electrical Plans & Documents

  • Electrical plans (layout, single-line diagram, panel schedule) in 5 sets
  • Specifications and load schedule
  • Bill of Materials for electrical works
  • Design Analysis signed by Licensed Electrical Engineer
C

Professional Credentials

  • PRC License (RME/REE) and PTR of signing engineer
  • IIEE Membership certificate (current year)
  • Signed Electrical Permit Application (EPC Form)

Fencing Permit Requirements

Required for the installation or modification of fencing around properties.

A

Basic Requirements

  • Accomplished Sanitary / Plumbing Permit Application Form
  • Proof of property ownership or written authorization
  • Copy of approved Building Permit
B

Technical Documents

  • Sanitary / Plumbing plans and specifications (5 sets)
  • Isometric diagram of the plumbing system
  • Bill of Materials for plumbing works
  • Signed by Licensed Sanitary Engineer or Master Plumber
